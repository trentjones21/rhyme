Place

Pick a room from the thumb strip, rotate if you need to, then tap empty floor that kisses the station. Blueprints are not yet walkable.

Assign

White kapsels work where you point them. Tap a room to send the nearest idle kapsel. Tap Recall to pull one home. They haul, build, cook, and man guns themselves.

Feed & mine

Gardens grow. Extractors only bite pink fields. Later kitchens cook sludge into pantry meals. Crew eat from the core, not from cargo in transit.

Hold

Waves come from the void. Weapons are mute until staffed. New worlds add flares, relics, gates, ice, cloaks, gravity, and overclocks — each taught in the station that introduces it.

Cloak

World 7 scouts hide until a staffed scanner sings. Guns cannot lead what they cannot see. Unseen locks the board until you tap Scan.

Gravity

Wells pull scouts into a current. Place a gun where bodies bunch — Well Trap is a kill lane, not a fair fight on the plus.

Overload

Select Over, then tap a staffed extractor or garden. It screams and doubles, then injures itself. Idle mining is not an overload.

Feed first

Gardens keep growing meals even if the kitchen is late. Relics do not feed anyone. On Last Geometry, plant garden and kitchen before you survey monuments, and never keep two unpaid blueprints.

Hull jobs

Scan sees cloaked scouts. Gun shoots what Scan reveals. Aegis eats flares. Two unpaid blueprints jam haulers — finish one dashed room before you stamp another. Last Geometry holds time until you tap GO.

The bag

Late stations deal a random tetromino. You still pick the room’s job. Rotate, or Hold to park a stubborn I or O. Unplaceable pieces are skipped. Add to Home Screen on iPhone for a portrait shell with haptics.
